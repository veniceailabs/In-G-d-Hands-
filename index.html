Privacy, in plain language

Your space stays yours.

You do not need an account to use In Göd Hands. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, health profile, contacts, or location to use the check-in, practices, or Honey.

By default

Your selected feeling, reflection writing, practice progress, and Honey messages are not saved to an In Göd Hands profile or chat history. A Honey message is sent only to generate its reply.

Only when you ask the team

We store only the contact details and note you choose to send, so the team can respond. The form makes this clear before anything is submitted.

About a private space

It is optional. It creates a random technical account ID without asking for personal information. It is tied to this browser session and cannot be recovered after you clear browser data or change devices.

If you choose anonymous feedback

We would save only a practice category and an optional three-choice response. We would not include your name, private-space ID, writing, Honey messages, contact information, or device identifier.

What technical information is involved?

Like nearly every website, our hosting, security, and database services may process limited technical information such as an IP address and browser data to run the service and prevent abuse. We do not use this to build a wellness profile or sell your information.

Create an optional private space

Today, it simply gives you a private, no-personal-info identity for features you explicitly choose to save in the future. All current practices remain unsaved by default.

One quick security check

It helps protect this anonymous space from automated sign-ups. It does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or wellness information.
